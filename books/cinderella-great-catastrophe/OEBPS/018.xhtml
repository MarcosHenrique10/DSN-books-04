When Lady Tremaine and the others arrived, Cinderella had
 dinner on the table.

“Is this the servant you were telling me about? I’d say she
 earned the right to go to the flower show,” Lady DuPont
 declared.

“I suppose so,” Lady Tremaine said reluctantly.

“Thank you for watching Precious and Treasure,” Lady
 DuPont said. “I know they can be a handful.”

“Not at all,” Cinderella said. “Actually, they lent me a hand.”
