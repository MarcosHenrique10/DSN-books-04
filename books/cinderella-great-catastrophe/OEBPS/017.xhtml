“Cinderelly to the rescue!” squeaked the mice.
 They sighed in relief once Cinderella had placed
 them in her apron pocket.

Cinderella heard a noise behind her. The cats
 had locked the window shut! There were no other
 windows Cinderella could reach. The roof was too
 steep to climb down. And dinner was about to
 burn!

Then, Cinderella noticed the clothesline
 stretching from the windowsill to a tree in the
 yard. Cinderella took off her apron and looped it
 around the clothesline.

“Everybody hold on tight!” she said. “Next
 stop, the kitchen!”
