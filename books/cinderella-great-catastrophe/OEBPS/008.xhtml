Cinderella gently placed the two cats on the sofa in the
 sitting room. Lucifer, the family cat, followed her.

“How much trouble could two sweet little kitty cats be?” she
 said.

Cinderella went to work. She dusted, scrubbed the floor, and
 put a roast into the oven. Then she went outside to wash the
 clothes.

“All done!” she said. “Now I’ll just check on the cats.”
