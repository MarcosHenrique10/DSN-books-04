As the footman carried
 the slipper to Cinderella, the
 Stepmother tripped him, and he
 fell! The slipper smashed into
 a hundred pieces. The Grand
 Duke was horrified!

Cinderella smiled and reached
 into her pocket. “Perhaps I can
 help. You see, I have the other
 slipper,” she said.

The Stepmother and
 stepsisters gasped. With a low
 bow, the Grand Duke slipped it
 onto Cinderella’s dainty foot. It
 fit perfectly!
