The next day, the King sent out a royal proclamation. It said that
 every maiden in the kingdom must try on the glass slipper—and the
 girl whose foot fit would wed the Prince!

Cinderella couldn’t hide her happiness from her stepmother. “So
 Cinderella is the girl the Prince seeks,” said Lady Tremaine, smiling
 cruelly. “Well, he’ll never find her!” And with that, she locked
 Cinderella in her attic room.

“Please! You can’t do this! Please, let me out!” cried Cinderella.

But the Stepmother ignored Cinderella.
