All that day, Cinderella’s stepmother and stepsisters shouted
 orders at her. Cinderella’s mouse and bird friends watched sadly.
 “Poor Cinderelly,” said the mice. “They keeping her so busy she
 never get her dress done.”

Then they had an idea! “We can do it!” the mice cheered.
 Soon they were happily snipping and stitching to make a lovely
 dress for Cinderella.
