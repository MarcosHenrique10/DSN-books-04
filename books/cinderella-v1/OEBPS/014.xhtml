“First we need a pumpkin and some mice,” said the Fairy
 Godmother. “Now for the magic words: bibbidi-bobbidi-boo!”
 With a wave of her wand, the Fairy Godmother turned the
 pumpkin into a coach and the mice into white horses.

The Fairy Godmother hurried Cinderella to her coach.
 “But . . . but . . . my dress,” Cinderella stammered.
