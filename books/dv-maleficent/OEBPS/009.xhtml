Alas, Maleficent’s raven was on the lookout for just such a thing.
 Spying sparks of magic coming from the chimney, he swooped toward
 the cottage. He watched as Aurora returned and told the good fairies
 about a handsome man she had met in the woods. She was in love!
 When the fairies told Aurora the truth—that she was really the
 princess—the Raven knew he had found the girl he was looking for.
 With a squawk, he flew off to tell Maleficent.
