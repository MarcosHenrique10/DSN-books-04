The thorny bushes began to burn from Maleficent’s fire. With
 flames rising all around him, Phillip needed to move to higher ground.
 He climbed onto a cliff, out of the fire.

Maleficent charged after him.

Phillip raised his sword and shield, prepared to continue the battle.

Maleficent reared up, poised to end Prince Phillip once and for
 all. But Phillip was ready for her. Seeing his moment, he took aim and
 threw his sword, striking Maleficent in the chest.

She lunged toward him but stumbled and fell off the cliff to her
 death.
