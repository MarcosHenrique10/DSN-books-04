Maleficent stepped closer to the boy. “Isn’t this a pleasant
 surprise,” she said. “I set my trap for a peasant, and lo, I catch a
 prince!” She had discovered that the boy was none other than Prince
 Phillip. He and Aurora had been promised to each other as children.

Maleficent had Phillip thrown into her dungeon. She then
 revealed to him that the girl he had fallen in love with was Princess
 Aurora and that she was under a spell only True Love’s Kiss could
 break.
