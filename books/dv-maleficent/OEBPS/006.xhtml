Maleficent cackled. Then, before the king’s guards could seize her,
 she disappeared in another flash of green fire.

The king was worried. Maleficent had great power, and he
 did not doubt that her curse would come true. He decided to send his
 daughter away to be cared for by the good fairies Flora, Fauna, and
 Merryweather.

The fairies wasted no time. They disguised themselves as peasants
 and took the baby where no one would find her.
