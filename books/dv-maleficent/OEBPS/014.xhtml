But Aurora was not dead, for the fairy Merryweather had softened
 the curse. At the touch of the spindle, Aurora fell into a deep sleep.
 Only True Love’s Kiss could awaken her.

Maleficent’s raven had heard the girl tell the fairies about the boy
 she had met in the forest. He was to meet her at the cottage that night.

Maleficent traveled to the cottage in the woods, where she lay in
 wait. When the boy stepped through the door, Maleficent’s henchmen
 attacked. The boy tried to get away, but the goons were too strong.
 They grabbed him and tied him up.
