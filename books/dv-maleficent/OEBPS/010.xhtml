At the castle, King Stefan and the Queen awaited their daughter’s
 return. It was Aurora’s sixteenth birthday. When the sun set, the
 princess would be free of Maleficent’s curse.

The fairies took Aurora to the castle. In her room, the girl asked
 to be left alone. Thinking her safe, the fairies agreed. But Aurora
 was not alone. Maleficent was waiting for her. A glowing green orb
 appeared, putting the princess into a trance. Unable to stop herself,
 Aurora followed the orb.
