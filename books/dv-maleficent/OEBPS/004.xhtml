I n a faraway land, long ago, a king and his fair queen longed for a
child. Finally, their wish was granted. They named their daughter
Aurora, after the dawn, for she filled their lives with sunshine.

From her home high on the Forbidden Mountain, Maleficent
watched as the king’s subjects arrived at the castle to celebrate the birth
of the princess. Maleficent had not been invited to the celebration, and
this made her angry.

In a flash of green fire, she transported herself to the castle, where
the three good fairies were busy bestowing gifts upon the girl.
