Sixteen years passed, and the princess grew into a beautiful, happy
 girl named Briar Rose. The girl did not know that she was really
 Princess Aurora and that her “aunts” were the good fairies.

On the Forbidden Mountain, storm clouds swirled and lightning
 crackled. “It’s incredible. Sixteen years and not a trace of her,”
 Maleficent snarled. “She couldn’t have vanished into thin air!”

When Maleficent’s henchmen assured her they had searched every
 cradle in the village, she could not contain her rage. They had been
 searching for a baby, when the princess was now grown!
