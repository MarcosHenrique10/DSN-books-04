Maleficent turned to her raven. “You are my last hope,” she
 said. “Circle far and wide. Search for a maid of sixteen with hair of
 sunshine gold and lips red as the rose. Go, and do not fail me.”

In the woods, the good fairies were preparing a celebration for
 Aurora’s sixteenth birthday. They had long before put away their
 wands, fearful that any magic would draw Maleficent to them. But
 while the young girl went for a walk in the woods, they withdrew
 them. What harm could a little magic do, when they had protected
 the princess for so long?
