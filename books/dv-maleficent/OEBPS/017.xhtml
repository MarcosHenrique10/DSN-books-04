Soon Phillip came to a clearing. He found Maleficent waiting for
 him. “Now shall you deal with me, oh prince,” she cried. In a burst of
 magic, Maleficent turned herself into a fire-breathing dragon.

Phillip charged forward, ready to face the menacing creature.
 With only the Shield of Virtue to protect him from the heat and force
 of Maleficent’s terrible flames, he slowly made his way forward.
