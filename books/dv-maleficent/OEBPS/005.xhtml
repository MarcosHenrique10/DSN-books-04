“I really felt quite distressed at not receiving an invitation,”
 Maleficent told the king. “To show I bear no ill will, I too shall
 bestow a gift on the child. Listen well. The princess shall indeed grow
 in grace and beauty, beloved by all who know her. But before the sun
 sets on her sixteenth birthday, she shall prick her finger on the spindle
 of a spinning wheel . . . and die!”
