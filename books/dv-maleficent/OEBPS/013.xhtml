When the fairies finally reached the room, they found Maleficent
 waiting for them.

“You poor simple fools,” Maleficent said, “thinking you could
 defeat me. Me! The mistress of all evil! Well, here’s your precious
 princess!”

Maleficent swept her cloak aside, revealing the princess’s still
 body. Then, with a wicked cackle, Maleficent disappeared.
