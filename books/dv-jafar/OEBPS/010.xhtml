Jafar watched triumphantly as Aladdin entered the cave. Soon the
 lamp would be his!

Suddenly, the ground began to rumble and shake. Jafar cursed
 under his breath. The boy must have touched something other than
 the lamp!

Jafar stepped to the cave’s entrance and peered inside. Aladdin
 was hanging from a rock. The ground was collapsing all around him.
 Aladdin begged the old man for help.

“First give me the lamp,” Jafar said.
