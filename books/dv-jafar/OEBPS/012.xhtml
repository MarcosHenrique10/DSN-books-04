Back at the palace, Jafar schemed. His plan to get the magic lamp
 had failed, but there was still another way to gain power. According
 to the law, if a suitable prince could not be found for the princess, the
 Sultan himself could choose her groom.

Jafar used his serpent staff to hypnotize the Sultan again. “You will
 order the princess to marry me!” he said.

Just then, there was a commotion outside. Jafar stepped onto the
 balcony. Below him, a new prince rode into town. His name was Prince
 Ali. He was there to woo the princess.
