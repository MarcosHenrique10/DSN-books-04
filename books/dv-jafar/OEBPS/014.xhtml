The next day, Prince Ali showed up to the palace. Before Jafar
 could stop him, the prince revealed that Jafar had tried to kill him and
 had been using his serpent staff to control the Sultan!

As Prince Ali turned to face him, Jafar noticed a lamp in his pocket.
 Jafar gasped. Prince Ali was really Aladdin! He must have used the
 lamp to escape the Cave of Wonders.

The Sultan ordered his guards to seize Jafar. But Jafar escaped. He
 hid until Aladdin was alone in his room. Then he used his parrot, Iago,
 to lure Aladdin outside by mimicking Princess Jasmine’s voice. When
 Aladdin went looking for her, Iago stole the lamp and gave it to Jafar.
