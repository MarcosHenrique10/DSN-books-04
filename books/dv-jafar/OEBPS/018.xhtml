Then Aladdin said something that made Jafar stop in his tracks:
 “The Genie has more power than you’ll ever have!”

Jafar was about to strike Aladdin down, but then he realized the boy
 was right. Jafar was a powerful sorcerer, but it was the Genie who had
 granted him all that power. Infuriated, Jafar used his final wish.

“I wish to be an all-powerful genie!” he commanded.

Jafar was instantly transformed into a genie. Now there was no
 stopping him. He would rule not only the kingdom but the entire world!
