Jafar returned to Agrabah, determined to find the Diamond in
 the Rough. But to do so, he would need the Sultan’s Mystic Blue
 Diamond ring.

A few days later, the Sultan approached Jafar. “I am in desperate
 need of your wisdom,” he said. The Sultan explained that his
 daughter, Jasmine, refused to marry.

Jafar saw his chance. Smiling, he said, “Perhaps I could divine a
 solution to this thorny problem. But it would require the use of the
 Mystic Blue Diamond. . . .”

Jafar withdrew his serpent staff. Its eyes glowed red, hypnotizing
 the Sultan, who happily handed over his ring.
