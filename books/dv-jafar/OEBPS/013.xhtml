Jafar watched angrily as Prince Ali charmed the Sultan and Princess
 Jasmine. He couldn’t let this pesky prince ruin what he had worked so
 hard for. At Jafar’s command, the palace guards captured Ali.

“I’m afraid you’ve worn out your welcome, Prince Abooboo,” Jafar
 said. He chuckled as the guards tied a weight to Prince Ali’s feet and
 threw him into the sea. Princess Jasmine was running out of time to
 find a prince. Soon she would have no choice but to marry Jafar!
