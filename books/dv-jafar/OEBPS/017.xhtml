Jafar had tried to do things the nice way. It was time to end this.
 He imprisoned Jasmine in a giant hourglass. Then he decided to take
 care of Aladdin once and for all.

Jafar watched the boy grab a sword. “Are you afraid to fight me
 yourself, you cowardly snake?” Aladdin shouted.

Jafar laughed, unimpressed. “Perhaps you’d like to see how
 snakelike I can be,” he replied, turning himself into an enormous
 cobra. “You little fool. You thought you could defeat the most
 powerful being on Earth?”
