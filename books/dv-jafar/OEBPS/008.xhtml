The guards locked Aladdin and his pet monkey, Abu, in the
 dungeon. Spying on him, Jafar heard Aladdin muttering about the
 Sultan’s daughter. He was in love with her!

Jafar now knew how to make the boy do his bidding!

Disguising himself as a feeble old man, Jafar entered the dungeon.
 He offered to make Aladdin rich if he would do just one small errand.
 “There is a cave, boy. A cave of wonders, filled with treasures beyond
 your wildest dreams. Treasure enough to impress even your princess.”
