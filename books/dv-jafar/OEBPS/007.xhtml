In his secret chamber, Jafar used the Sultan’s diamond ring to
 activate the Sands of Time.

“Reveal to me the one who can enter the cave,” he commanded.

The sands showed an image of a poor boy named Aladdin.

“There he is,” Jafar rejoiced, “my Diamond in the Rough. Let’s
 have the guards extend an invitation to the palace, shall we?”

Looking into the hourglass, Jafar laughed evilly. Soon he would
 rule over all of Agrabah!
