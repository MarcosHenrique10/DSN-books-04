Jafar smiled slyly. After years of searching for it, finally the
 powerful lamp was within his grasp. As Gazeem moved closer to the
 entrance, the tiger head boomed, “Only one may enter here—the
 Diamond in the Rough!”

Suddenly, the tiger head entrance collapsed. Gazeem disappeared
 under the sand!

Jafar fumed. “I must find this one,” he said, “this Diamond in the
 Rough.”
