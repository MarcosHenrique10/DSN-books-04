Showing Aladdin a secret exit in the dungeon, Jafar led the boy to
 the Cave of Wonders.

“Who disturbs my slumber?” the tiger head entrance roared.

“It is I, Aladdin,” the boy replied.

“Proceed,” the tiger thundered.

“Remember, boy,” Jafar called as Aladdin stared up at the glowing
 cave. “First fetch me the lamp. Then you shall have your reward!”
