Next Jafar wished to be the most powerful sorcerer in the world.
 Then he exiled Aladdin far, far away.

Jafar cackled in delight. He was getting everything he had ever
 wanted. Even better, it seemed that Princess Jasmine was beginning to
 see things his way.

“Jafar,” she said, “I never realized how incredibly handsome you are.”

Jafar reached to return her embrace. But as he leaned closer, he saw a
 troubling reflection in her crown: Aladdin was back!
