A story of family and friendship

Oscar is a baby chimpanzee who lives in
the African rain forest. One day, his mother
disappears. He is all alone–until an older
chimp named Freddy notices him. Join
Oscar and Freddy as they become friends
and Freddy teaches Oscar how to survive.
