Elena doesn’t know what she
 would do without her
 three best friends.

She chose her friend Mateo to
 be Avalor’s Royal Wizard,
 just like his grandfather was.

Elena’s friend Gabe is on the
 Royal Guard and is always
 looking out for her. Sometimes she
 thinks he worries too much, but she
 knows she can count on him.

Naomi’s family is in
 charge of the ships that arrive in
 Avalor City, so she knows people
 from all over. Her understanding
 of the world is really helpful on
 the Grand Council.
