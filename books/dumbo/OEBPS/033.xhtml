One crow gave Timothy an ordinary feather and said,
 “This is a magic feather. It will help Dumbo fly!”

Holding the feather in his trunk, Dumbo stood at the
 edge of a cliff. . . .

Before he could change his mind, the crows pushed
 Dumbo and off he went. All at once, Dumbo was flapping
 his big ears—and he was flying!

“You did it!” cried Timothy.
