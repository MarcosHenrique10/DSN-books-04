The other clowns pretended to be firefighters. They
 sprayed Dumbo with water and held a hoop for him to
 jump into. Poor Dumbo was terrified! Down, down he
 dropped until he fell through the hoop . . . into a tub of
 sticky goo!

The audience cheered and roared with laughter. But
 Dumbo was not happy at all. To cheer him up, Timothy
 said, “We’ll go see your mother!”
