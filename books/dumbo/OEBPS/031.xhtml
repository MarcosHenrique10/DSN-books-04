Dumbo was so surprised, he lost his balance. He and
 Timothy tumbled into a pond below.

“I wonder how we got in that tree,” said Timothy, shaking
 himself dry.

“Maybe you flew!” a crow joked.

“That’s it!” cried Timothy. “Dumbo, you flew up there!”
 The little elephant looked surprised. He couldn’t really
 fly—could he?

“You just have to believe in yourself, Dumbo,” said
 Timothy.
