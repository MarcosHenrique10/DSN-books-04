Only male lions have manes.

Females do most of the hunting
for a pride.

Lions often hunt at night.

Lions spend sixteen to twenty
hours per day resting.

Lions can go up to four or five days without water.

A lion can’t roar until it is
around two years old.

A lion’s roar can be heard from
five miles away.

When a lion walks, its heels
don’t touch the ground.

Lions can run at speeds of up to thirty-five miles per
hour, but only for short distances.

At birth, the average weight of a
lion cub is two to four pounds.

A full-grown male lion can
weigh between 330 and 550
pounds.
