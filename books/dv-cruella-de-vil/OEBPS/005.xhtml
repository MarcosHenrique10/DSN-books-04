Cruella sauntered over to a picture of Anita’s dogs. “Such
 beautifully perfect coats,” she muttered to herself. Cruella turned
 back to Anita. “The puppies,” she said. “Where are the little brutes?”

“Oh, it will be at least three weeks,” Anita replied.

Cruella sighed. She was not used to waiting for anything. But
 beauty was worth the wait. And Cruella was sure the Dalmatian
 puppies’ fur would make a beautiful coat!

“I’ve got to run, darling,” she told Anita. “Let me know when the
 puppies arrive. You will, won’t you, dear? Don’t forget. It’s a promise.
 See you in three weeks!”
