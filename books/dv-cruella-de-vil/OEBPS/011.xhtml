At the De Vil place, Horace and Jasper were lazily watching TV. All
 around them were Dalmatians. They had done as Cruella asked and
 purchased more puppies. Now they had a total of ninety-nine pups!

Suddenly, Cruella stormed in. The police were looking for the pups.
 She had to act fast. “It’s got to be done tonight!” she said. “Do you
 understand? Tonight!”

“But they ain’t big enough,” Jasper said.

Cruella was furious. She wanted her coat, and she wanted it now!
