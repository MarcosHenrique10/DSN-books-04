But it was not to be. Instead of crashing into the grocery truck,
 Cruella collided with Horace and Jasper. Both of their cars went over
 an embankment.

“You idiots!” Cruella called, stepping from the wreckage. “You
 fools.”

Looking up, Cruella saw the truck driving safely away. The
 Dalmatian puppies were free!

Cruella fumed. She had lost her chance . . . for now.
