Three weeks passed slowly as Cruella waited for word that the
 puppies had been born. Each night she went to bed dreaming of her
 luxurious new coat, but still no call came from Anita. Then, one wild
 and stormy night, the phone rang.

Delighted, Cruella rushed over to Anita’s home. At last, she would
 have her fur! But when she looked at the puppies, she grew quite
 angry. “They’re mongrels!” she cried. “No spots! No spots at all.
 What a horrid little white rat!”

“They’ll get their spots in a few weeks,” Anita explained.

At that, Cruella cheered up. “In that case, I’ll take them all!”
