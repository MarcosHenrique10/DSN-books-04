Cruella drove through the night. It was snowing, and the road was
 becoming hard to see, but she didn’t care. A little bad weather wasn’t
 about to stop her from getting what she wanted!

As the sun rose, Cruella noticed paw prints on the ground. At last,
 she had found a clue. “They thought they could outwit Cruella,” she
 said with an evil laugh.

Following the tracks, Cruella made her way to a small village.
 Surely she would find her quarry there!

But search as she might, Cruella could not find a single
 Dalmatian. All she saw were black Labradors, everywhere she looked.
