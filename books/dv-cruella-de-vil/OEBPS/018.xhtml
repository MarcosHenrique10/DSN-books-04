Cruella stepped as hard as she
 could on the gas and took off after
 the grocery truck. As she caught
 up with it, she rammed into it
 from the side. Her car bounced off
 the truck, and she steered to hit it
 again.

Cruella was determined. If she
 could send the truck over the cliff,
 the puppies would have no way to
 escape. She would capture them
 again and finally get her coat!

Cruella was so focused on
 the grocery truck that she wasn’t
 watching the road. She drove
 straight off a cliff, and her car
 came to a stop in a pile of snow.
 Reversing out of the snow,
 she raced back onto the road,
 determined to catch up with the
 truck.
