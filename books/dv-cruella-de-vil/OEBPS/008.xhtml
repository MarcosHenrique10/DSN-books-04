“I’m afraid we can’t give them up,” Anita said.

Cruella thought for a moment. She had no lack of money. Surely
 she could make it worth Anita’s while to sell the puppies. Taking out
 her checkbook, she said, “I’ll pay you twice what they’re worth.”

Cruella began to write out the check, but her pen would not work.
 As she shook it, ink sprayed all over Roger!

“We’re not selling the puppies,” Roger told her. “And that’s final!”

Cruella could hardly believe her ears. No one said no to her!
 “Fine. Keep the little beasts for all I care,” she shouted. “But I warn
 you, Anita. I’ll get even.”
