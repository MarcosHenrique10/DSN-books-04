At home, Cruella fumed. If she couldn’t buy the pups, she’d just
 have to find another way to get at their beautiful fur!

Cruella waited until she was sure the pups would have their spots.
 Then she sent her two minions, Horace and Jasper, to Anita’s house.
 When Anita and Roger left for a walk, the two rang the doorbell.
 Nanny answered, and they shoved past her into the house. Then, after
 locking Nanny up, they snatched all fifteen puppies!
