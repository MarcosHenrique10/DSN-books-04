The next evening, Cruella laughed wickedly to herself as she read
 the paper. “Dognapping. Can you imagine such a thing?”

Just then, her phone rang. It was Jasper. He wanted the money
 Cruella had promised him for stealing the dogs.

But Cruella had more work for Jasper to do. After all, fifteen
 puppies were a good start, but that was not nearly enough to make the
 coat she had been dreaming about!

“Not one shilling until the job is done!” Cruella shouted.
